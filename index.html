About me

Senior backend engineer from Siena, Italy. I've spent the last 9 years on the same enterprise platform, growing from junior developer to technical owner of the backend handling conversations and the back-office.

I also look after on-premise installations for banking customers, with strict high-availability requirements: releases there happen without the users ever noticing - that's the whole point.

I care about databases, high availability, observability and privacy by design. I also strongly believe that a good senior engineer deletes more code than they write.

I'm just the friendly Italian software engineer in the neighborhood, and I still love coding in Java

Spoken languages: Italian and English
